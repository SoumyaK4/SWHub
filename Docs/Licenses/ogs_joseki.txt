SWHub offers a separately downloaded, transformed offline snapshot of the Online-Go.com (OGS)
Joseki Explorer position graph. The snapshot contains move sequences, move
classifications, position descriptions, board marks, tags, links, and joseki
source metadata obtained from the public OGS Joseki API. Public discussion
fields and contributor identifiers are not included in the download.
